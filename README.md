## 🚀 Featured Projects

<table>

<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🐟 AquaSense
**IoT + ML water quality monitoring for aquaculture**

ESP32 sensors track pH, turbidity and temperature in fish ponds. An LSTM forecasts unsafe conditions *hours before* they happen, and Gemini turns the prediction into plain Sinhala advice for the farmer. Adds predator detection via computer vision and automated aerator/pump control.

`ESP32` `LSTM` `Gemini API` `React` `Flask`

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🌱 RootWatch
**Smart irrigation & plant health advisor**

Soil moisture, light and leaf-colour sensing on an ESP32-C6, with an LSTM predicting irrigation needs and a solenoid valve that waters automatically (with a safety auto-close timer). Gemini Vision analyses plant photos for early signs of stress.

`ESP32-C6` `TensorFlow` `Flask` `React` `Flutter`

</td>
</tr>

`Flutter` `Dart` `Gemini API` `Speech-to-Text`

**Deep learning change detection**

A DNN research project that detects and highlights visual differences between two similar images or video frames — for surveillance, quality inspection and frame-by-frame film comparison. Comparing YOLO, Siamese ResNet and EfficientNet approaches.

`YOLO` `PyTorch` `OpenCV` `Siamese Networks`

`React` `Node.js` `PostgreSQL` `Gemini API`
